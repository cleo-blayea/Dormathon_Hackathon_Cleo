## Root Cause Analysis

### What the Data Confirms (Observed Facts)

1. **The generator engine and alternator are healthy.** Voltage was nominal (399--402V), frequency was nominal (49.9--50.2Hz), oil pressure was normal (3.69--4.18 bar), coolant temperature was normal (63--73C), battery voltage was healthy (22.8--27.9V).

2. **The ATS controller logic is functioning.** It correctly detected the utility failure, commanded the generator to start, and commanded source = GENERATOR. The controller's decision-making is not the problem.

3. **The ATS did not transfer the building load.** Despite the controller commanding GENERATOR, the actual position remained UTILITY for all 11 failure records. Current was 0.0A throughout. The zero current, position mismatch, and failed transfer confirmations are consistent with unsuccessful transfer -- no electrical connection was made between the generator and the building load.

4. **The generator stopped itself for safety.** After 10 minutes at no load, the DSE6120 controller stopped the engine. This is expected behavior, not an engine fault.

### Suspected Root Causes (Not Independently Confirmed)

Based on the observed evidence, the most likely root causes, in order of probability:

**1. ATS contactor/actuator mechanical failure (MOST LIKELY)**
The controller sent the command, but the contactor did not move. This is consistent with:
- Mechanical binding or seizure of the contactor mechanism
- Actuator motor or solenoid failure
- Mechanical linkage breakage

**2. Control wiring fault between controller and actuator**
The controller's command may not have reached the actuator. This is consistent with:
- Loose or corroded wiring connection
- Broken control wire
- Failed intermediate relay

**3. Position sensor fault (LESS LIKELY)**
The sensor may be reporting UTILITY incorrectly even if the contactor moved. However, **0.0A current** throughout makes this less likely -- if the contactor had actually transferred, current would flow even if the sensor reported incorrectly. The zero current is consistent with no transfer occurring.

### Unverified Assumptions

1. Whether the ATS contactor is motor-operated, solenoid-operated, or spring-loaded -- not specified in available data
2. Whether the "ATS contact inspection" performed on 2026-05-15 was visual or functional -- maintenance record says "ATS contact inspection" but does not specify test method
3. Whether a facility single-line diagram is available to verify ATS topology
4. Load-bus voltage data is not in the dataset -- facility power status is inferred from the alarm field only

### What This Is NOT

- **NOT an engine failure** -- the engine started and ran normally
- **NOT an alternator failure** -- voltage and frequency were nominal
- **NOT a fuel problem** -- fuel level was 427.9L (82% of tank)
- **NOT a battery problem** -- battery was 22.8--27.9V (healthy)
- **NOT a controller logic error** -- the controller made all correct decisions
